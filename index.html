How to Play

Keyboard

  • WASD / Arrows — move
  • Shift — sprint
  • Z — pass
  • X — through ball / lob
  • C (hold) — shoot (release to strike, hold longer = more power)
  • Space — tackle / slide when defending
  • Q — switch player
  • Esc — pause

Touch

  • Left stick — move
  • SPR — sprint
  • PASS — ground pass
  • CROSS — through ball / lob
  • TACKLE — tackle / slide when defending
  • SHOT (hold & release) — shoot

You always control the outfield player nearest the ball. Goalkeepers are automatic.

Outside mode is a 4-a-side pickup game on a small walled court — the ball bounces off the boards instead of going out, so it's just kickoffs and goals, no stoppages.
